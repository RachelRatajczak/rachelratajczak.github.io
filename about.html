My Approach

01

Scope & Validate

02

Analyze

Apply rigorous statistical techniques using Python, SQL, or R to uncover patterns, evaluate clinical metrics, and test hypotheses within complex health datasets.

03

Visualize

Transform multi-dimensional metrics into clean, compelling dashboards in Tableau or Power BI that make intricate findings instantly clear.

04

Translate

Bridge the gap between technical data and clinical operations, delivering actionable insights that support multidisciplinary teams and improve decision-making.
